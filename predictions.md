## 2. My findings

### Finding 1
- Where: Second page — Reporting Technician section
- What I did, what I saw: I tried to edit the Reporting Technician information, but this section could not be edited.
- Which heuristic: #3 User Control and Freedom
- Screen or system: Screen. The interface shows the technician information, but the user cannot change it when needed.
- Severity, and why: 3 — Major usability problem. If the technician information is incorrect or needs to be changed, the user cannot correct it.
- The repair: Allow the Reporting Technician field to be edited, or clearly explain why it is locked and how the user can change it.

### Finding 2
- Where: All three main pages
- What I did, what I saw: When I first entered the product, I saw a large amount of information across the main pages and it was not immediately clear what I should do first.
- Which heuristic: #8 Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
- Screen or system: Screen. Too much information competes for attention and makes the main workflow less obvious.
- Severity, and why: 3 — Major usability problem. A first-time user may need extra time to understand where to start and what action to take.
- The repair: Reduce or reorganize the information and create a clearer visual hierarchy so that the primary action is immediately visible.

### Finding 3
- Where: Main workflow / all three main pages
- What I did, what I saw: As a first-time user, I could not find clear instructions or help explaining how to use the product or what steps to follow.
- Which heuristic: #10 Help and Documentation
- Screen or system: Screen. There is no clear onboarding, help text, or guidance for a new user.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Minor usability problem. The product can still be used, but a new user may need to explore the interface before understanding the workflow.
- The repair: Add short onboarding instructions, contextual help, or a simple guide showing the main workflow.

### Finding 4
- Where: Error states in the main workflow
- What I did, what I saw: When something goes wrong, the interface does not clearly explain what happened or tell the user what to do next.
- Which heuristic: #9 Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors
- Screen or system: System. Error feedback does not provide enough information for the user to understand or recover from the problem.
- Severity, and why: 3 — Major usability problem. Users may become stuck because they do not know what caused the problem or how to recover.
- The repair: Show clear error messages that explain what happened and provide a specific next step or recovery action.
